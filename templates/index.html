Strategy Glossary

Terms used on this page
Undercut
Pitting before a rival to use fresh, grippier tyres and pass them while they're still out on old ones.
Overcut
Pitting later than a rival — pushing on old tyres, then rejoining in clear air on fresh ones.
Stint
A continuous run on one set of tyres, until the next pit stop.
Tyre Degradation
How much slower lap times get as tyres age, in seconds per lap per lap of tyre life.
Pit Window
The range of laps where pitting gives the best total race time, per the grid search.
Pace Offset
A per-lap constant that calibrates the model so simulated time matches that driver's real time.
MAE / RMSE
The model's average error against real lap times (seconds per lap) — lower is more accurate.
Safety Car / VSC
Periods of track-wide speed limits after an incident — often changing whether a pit stop is worth it.
Grid Position
The driver's real starting position, used as one input to the race simulation.
